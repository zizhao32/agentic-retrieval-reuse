(English translation of GUIDE_zh.txt, which is the version annotators used.)

Each item shows a question and a set of retrieved passages. Judge only from these
passages: do they contain all the facts needed to answer the question?
- Consider only what the passages contain; do not fill gaps with your own knowledge.
  Anything not stated explicitly counts as "not sufficient".
- The order and numbering of the passages do not matter.
- If you are unsure, choose "unsure"; do not guess.
Do not discuss items with other annotators and do not look at any model's verdicts
while annotating.
